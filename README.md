# Books and Courses

| [back](../readme.md) | [learning Log](./log.md) | 

I'll study these books segment-by-segment and with great care. if there is any 
specific video course available, I will watch all of its videos carefully.

My goal is to learn classical computer science topics and to gain proficiency 
in low-level programming ( such as C and Rust ) for developing useful tools and 
operating systems, for debugging, hacking, and for security.

For now, I am designing and studying the basic section, and in the future—at
the appropriate time—I will complete the other sections.

Here we have two threads, and I plan to study both concurrently, dedicating 
time to each every day, over the course of four years. Each thread also consists 
of two packages, with roughly two years to be spent on each package.
